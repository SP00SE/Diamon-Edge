STREAK LABELS

Compares a player's last 7 games batting average (L7 AVG) to their full season AVG. The difference is measured in batting average points.

🔥 Hot StreakL7 AVG is 30+ points higher than season AVG — player is on fire recently
❄️ Cold StreakL7 AVG is 30+ points lower than season AVG — player is in a recent slump
— SteadyL7 AVG is within 29 points of season AVG — performing close to their normal level

Example: Season AVG .280, L7 AVG .220 → difference = −60 pts → ❄️ Cold Streak

Hot Streaks Tab — Flame Intensity shown on streak cards

The number of 🔥 (hits) or 💥 (HRs) on a streak card shows how hot the streak is.

🔥🔥🔥 HitL7 AVG is 80+ points above season AVG — elite recent form
🔥🔥 HitL7 AVG is 50–79 points above season AVG — very hot
🔥 HitL7 AVG is up to 49 points above season AVG — on a streak
💥💥💥 HR3+ home runs in the last 7 games
💥💥 HR2 home runs in the last 7 games

BABIP Batting Average on Balls In Play

Measures how often a batter gets a hit when they put the ball in play — home runs and strikeouts are excluded. League average is typically around .290–.300. It's one of the best indicators of whether a batter's results reflect true skill or luck.

.330+Elite — balls in play are becoming hits at an exceptional rate. Strong contact quality or a hot stretch
.290–.329Average — results are right in line with league norms
<.265Below average — balls in play are turning into outs more than expected. Could signal weak contact or unlucky bounces

Example: A batter hitting .220 with a BABIP of .340 is likely due for positive regression — they're making solid contact but the balls aren't falling in yet.

BACON Batting Average on CONtact

Similar to BABIP but includes home runs — measures how often a batter gets a hit whenever they make contact (strikeouts excluded). A high BACON means the batter consistently converts contact into hits, which is a strong sign of a dangerous hitter.

.320+Elite contact hitter — almost every ball put in play is a threat
.280–.319Average — solid contact rate, no major red flags
<.265Below average — making contact but not converting. Weak contact or bad luck

For pitchers, these stats are flipped — a low BABIP/BACON allowed means batters struggle to get hits even on contact, which signals elite stuff.

BB/BF% Walk Rate per Batter Faced

Measures how often a pitcher walks a batter out of every batter they face. A high BB/BF% means the pitcher struggles with command and gives free passes — a direct advantage for hitters.

< 6%Elite command — pitcher rarely walks anyone
7–11%Average to below-average command
> 12%Wild — struggles to throw strikes, hitters benefit from free passes

Used in The Lab matchup score: high BB/BF% adds up to +6 pts for the hitter; elite command subtracts up to −6 pts.

GHP Games with Hit Percentage

The percentage of games in which a player records at least one hit. A consistency metric popular in MLB Beat the Streak — a player who hits in 70% of games is far more reliable than one who goes 0-for-4 half the time.

≥ 70%Elite — gets a hit in 7 out of every 10 games
55–69%Average — solid but not fully consistent
< 55%Inconsistent — frequent hitless games, risky for streak play

Shown on The Lab hitter card and factors into the Lab matchup score (up to ±7 pts).
